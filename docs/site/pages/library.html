Library

The Library panel keeps processed maps side by side, so you can plot them again, join measurements of different spectral or field ranges, or average repeated sweeps.

Adding maps

  • Save current map adds the R(B)/R(0) map shown: the processed sweep, or a merged or averaged result.
  • Load table… adds data tables exported earlier (Ctrl+E; see the format). You can drop tables on the panel, too.
  • Field from the table header reads the fields from the table's header (0.25T, 0.50T, …); off, loaded tables take the Sample panel's custom field range.

There is no limit to the number of maps.

Plotting and limits

Each map has a tick, its name, a Plot button and a button that opens its limits: E min and E max (in the unit shown) and B min and B max (in tesla). An empty limit means no cut.

Plot shows the map on the plots. With Plot the full energy range off, it is cut to its E limits first. While a library map is shown, the status bar says Showing a library map; you can view it, pick on it, fit it and export it like a processed sweep.

Merging and averaging

Tick the maps to combine; the buttons need at least two. Each ticked map is first cut to its own limits.

Merge by energy
Joins maps measured in different spectral ranges, such as FIR and MIR, each cut to its E limits, and puts the result on one energy axis with a uniform step.
Merge by field
Joins maps measured over different field ranges, such as 0 – 8 T and 8 – 16 T, each cut to its B limits. Fields measured in both are averaged.
Average
Averages repeated measurements of the same fields, each cut to its E and B limits.

The result is shown like a library map. To keep it, export it as a data table, or save it into the library again.
