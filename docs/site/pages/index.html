From an FTIR field sweep to a journal figure

Magneto-Optical Detective is a desktop app for magneto-optical spectroscopy. Load a field sweep measured with Bruker OPUS, look at it as R(B)/R(0) maps and stacked spectra, pick the Landau-level transitions, fit a model to them and export the figure at the size the journal asks for.

What it does

One window for the whole analysis of a sweep, from the raw spectra to the fitted parameters and the printed figure.

  • Maps from a sweep

    Drop a folder of OPUS files or OPUS-macro text files. The fields are read from the file names, the zero-field drift is corrected, and Process draws R(B)/R(0), the data, R(B)/R(B-AVR) and R(B)/R(B-ΔB), with 1st and 2nd derivatives along energy or field. Processing

  • Live units, clear colours

    Switch between cm⁻¹, meV and THz at any time: maps, axes, ranges, picked points, models and exports change at once, without processing again. Seven colour maps, with levels kept per plot. Viewing

  • Pick the transitions

    Click the map to record a transition energy at a field, or let auto-pick follow a line field by field or find every line in a region. Every edit can be undone. Picking points

  • Fit models

    Massive Dirac transitions, Zeeman or magnon branches with optional coupling, or your own expression in B. Fit them to the picked curves and read every parameter as value ± σ, with χ². Models and fitting

  • Journal figures

    Nature, APS or your own sizes in mm, text and lines in pt. PDF, SVG and EPS keep their text editable; PNG and TIFF carry the dpi. The preview follows the main window live. Journal figures

  • Combine measurements

    Keep processed maps in the library, then join spectral ranges such as FIR and MIR, join field ranges, or average repeated sweeps. Library

Good to know

  • Reads OPUS directlyOPUS binary files (*.0, *.1, …) or the two-column text files of the OPUS export macro.
  • Plain tables outMaps, points and fit results as tab-separated text, with the energy unit in the header.
  • Remembers your choicesUnits, ranges, colours and the window layout are kept between sessions.
  • Runs offlineEverything happens on your computer; the app needs no network connection.
