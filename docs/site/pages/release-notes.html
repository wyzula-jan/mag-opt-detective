Release notes

Version 5.0

Upcoming · not released

Version 5.0 is the first release of the rewritten app. It replaces the PyQt5 scripts up to version 4.9 and is still in development: this page describes the state of the main branch, and it changes until the release is tagged.

Highlights

  • A new workbench window, in light and dark, with sliding panels, an inspector and a log.
  • A live switch between cm⁻¹, meV and THz that converts everything without processing again.
  • Auto-pick: follow a line field by field, or find every line in a region.
  • Models and fitting: massive Dirac, Zeeman or magnon branches with coupling, and your own expressions, fitted to the picked points with errors.
  • Journal figures for Nature, APS or custom sizes, with editable text.
  • Standalone apps for macOS, Windows and Linux.

A new app

  • Rewritten from the ground up on Qt 6 (PySide6), numpy and scipy, as a Python package that runs with uv run mag-opt-detective.
  • Standalone apps that need no Python, built and checked for every release on macOS, Windows and Linux.
  • Automatic tests of the processing, the file formats and the window on all three systems.
  • Your choices (units, ranges, colours, layout and more) are remembered between sessions; View › Reset settings restores the defaults.

Loading and processing

  • OPUS binary files are read by the app itself, and OPUS and text files are told apart automatically.
  • Drop a whole sweep folder on the Sample panel: the files are sorted by field, zero-field files go to their own list, and missing fields are reported.
  • New plot R(B)/R(B-ΔB), the ratio of neighbouring field steps.
  • Derivatives per unit: d/dE per cm⁻¹, meV or THz and d/dB per tesla, besides per data point.
  • A dot on Process shows that settings changed since the last run.

Viewing

  • The energy unit is no longer chosen before processing: switch it at any time, and maps, axes, ranges, points, models and exports follow.
  • Field, energy and intensity ranges with Auto or Fixed, shared by the map and the stacked plot.
  • Colour maps magma, inferno, viridis, plasma, turbo, grey and bipolar; levels Auto, Fixed or Symmetric, remembered per plot kind; a histogram of the map.
  • Classic histogram colour scales or slim colour bars, each of which slides away.
  • Stacked spectra with an offset, every n-th field and colours by field.
  • Errors appear in a bar above the plot with a button that opens the panel to fix them, and the log keeps the details.

Picking

  • Curves as chips with their colours, a table of their points, and markers for the current curve, all curves or none.
  • Undo and redo for every point edit (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z); Alt-click removes a point.
  • Picking on the stacked plot, with the points drawn on the traces.
  • Auto-pick with Track and Detect, for maxima, minima or inflection points, with a preview to accept or discard.

Models and fitting

  • Massive Dirac transitions drawn over the map and fitted.
  • Zeeman and magnon branches, linear or hyperbolic, optionally coupled to give avoided crossings.
  • Custom expressions in B with physical constants, checked as you type.
  • Fits to the picked curves by branch, by energy order or to the nearest branch, with value ± σ for every parameter, χ², and results to copy or save as a table.

Library

  • The numbered slots of the old version became a library without a limit, with energy and field limits per map.
  • New: Merge by field (e.g. 0 – 8 T and 8 – 16 T) and Average of repeated sweeps, next to Merge by energy.

Export

  • The journal figure window: Nature, APS and custom presets, PDF, SVG and EPS with editable text, PNG and TIFF with their dpi, and a live preview.
  • Quick PNG or SVG images of the plot as shown, with its colour scale.
  • Fit results as tab-separated tables.

Changes and compatibility

  • Data tables keep the format of the old versions: tables from 4.9 load into the library, and new tables open where the old ones did.
  • Point tables now name the energy unit in their first header cell, e.g. Energy (meV). Tables from 4.9, without it, are read in the unit shown.
  • Ctrl+F still processes, next to the new Ctrl+Return; Ctrl+E, Ctrl+1 to 3 and Alt+1 to 3 work as before.

Known limitations

  • The apps are not code-signed: macOS and Windows ask once before the first launch (how to open them).
  • There is no build for Intel Macs; run from source there.
  • In the standalone app the first journal figure takes about 20 s while the font cache is built.
  • The two-column width of the APS preset (17.8 cm) is not confirmed by an official APS page.
  • Released under the GNU GPL v3; see how to cite the app in a publication.

Older versions

Versions up to 4.9 were PyQt5 scripts versioned by file name; the last of them is kept in git under the tag v4.9-legacy. Releases from 5.0 on are listed on GitHub.
